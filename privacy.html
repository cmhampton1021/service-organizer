Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Red Oak Media, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Car Group Organizer application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All data you enter — including names, group assignments, and car configurations — is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device.

2. Local Storage

The App uses your browser's localStorage API to persist your session data between visits. This data is stored locally on your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. You can clear this data at any time by:

  • Using the "Clear Session" button within the App
  • Clearing your browser's local storage through your browser settings

3. No Cookies

The App does not use cookies or any other tracking technologies.

4. No Third-Party Sharing

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no data to share with third parties. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer any information to outside parties.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since the App collects no data whatsoever, there is no risk of collecting children's information.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
